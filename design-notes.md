# Design Notes — Ethiopia Digital Inclusion

## Purpose
Document the main design and analytical decisions behind the Visual System, Insight Brief, and social media adaptations so the work is transparent and reusable.

## Analytical Approach
- Focused on decision-relevant patterns rather than exhaustive charting.
- Prioritised three stories: mobile vs internet adoption gap, mobile-first access structure, and the link between connectivity and economic output.
- Every visual is paired with a short plain-language insight that states what the numbers mean for the reader.

## Design Principles Applied
- Accuracy first, aesthetics second.
- Restrained institutional palette (Deep Slate Navy + Warm Terracotta accent).
- Direct labelling preferred over separate legends.
- Generous whitespace and consistent card structure for scannability.
- One-page constraint forced prioritisation of the strongest findings only.

## Tools & Process
- Analysis and data preparation: Excel / Google Sheets + supporting R exploration.
- Visual system and layouts: Figma.
- AI tools used for initial layout exploration and copy refinement; all calculations, sources, and final claims independently verified.

## What I Owned
- Data selection, cleaning, and validation
- Insight extraction and framing
- Visual system (colour, type, chart language, do/don’t rules)
- Full design of the Insight Brief and LinkedIn carousel
- Documentation of limitations and sources
